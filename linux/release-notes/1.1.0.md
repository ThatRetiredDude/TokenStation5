- Users. Give someone else their own account: the chat page and the API with their own keys, and only what you allow (web search, an app's own tools, tokens a day, the longest reply). Each user's chats are their own, on every device with their key; you can read them only if that was chosen when the user was made, and they see it in their Settings. Users never get ts5-tui or the browser terminal; an editor or any OpenAI-compatible app works with their key. The Access page (F6) lists users with their keys: U adds one, Enter changes what they may do, P pauses, D removes. Users are kept apart: nobody sees another's chats, and the prompt cache is never shared between users, so nothing in its speed or usage numbers tells one user what another asked; a browser two people share shows its old chats to the owner's key only, and the log never holds another user's search words.
- Downloaded models stay on the drive until you delete them. The Models page (F2) lists them as Downloaded models with each one's size, their size in all and what's free; Enter loads one without downloading it again, and D or Delete removes one, asked first (never the model loaded; its tuned settings stay, so a download of it again is ready at once).
